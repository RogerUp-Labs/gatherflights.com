How can we help?

Short version: GatherFlights puts your group's flights on one arrivals board. These short guides cover joining a trip, privacy and flight times.

An organizer makes a trip and shares one invite link. Everyone adds their own flight, and the arrivals board shows who has landed. A trip can be a group trip (everyone sees the board), a hosted trip like a wedding (only the host sees the list), or a solo trip (one traveler, with people following along).
