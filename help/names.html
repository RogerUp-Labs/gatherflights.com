Privacy in the app

How your names stay private

Short version: names are scrambled on your phone or computer before they reach us. Only the end of the invite link can unscramble them, and that part never reaches us.

How it works

  1. Names are scrambled before they leave your device. When you type your name, your phone or computer scrambles it before sending it to us. The same goes for the trip name and the trip info cards. (The technical word is encrypted.)

  2. The end of the invite link unscrambles them. An invite link has a part at the end, after the # sign. That's what turns the scrambled text back into names.

  3. Browsers never send that part to any website. It's how web browsers work: whatever comes after the # stays on your device. So it never reaches us.

  4. So all we ever store is scrambled text. We can't read the names in your trip, even if we wanted to.

  5. That's why the whole link matters. Without its end, names can't be read, so always send the whole invite link. Some apps cut long links short; if someone sees "This link is missing a piece", send it again. It's also why a new phone or computer needs an OK from a device that already has the trip, for example with "Add a device".

Only send the link to people you mean to invite. Anyone with the link can join the trip.

Your email address

If you organize trips, your email address isn't stored either. We keep a coded version that can only confirm it's you when you sign in again.

What we can still see

To follow flights and run your trip, some things can't be scrambled. We can see:

  • Flight numbers, dates and airports, and each flight's live status.
  • How many people are in a trip, and how many flights.
  • When the trip ends, so we know when to delete it.

If you turn on "Hide my flight from the others", the others see you as +1, but your flight still reaches us so we can follow it.

Our flight information provider gets flight numbers and dates, never names. See what we keep, and for how long.
