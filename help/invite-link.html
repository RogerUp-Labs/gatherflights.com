GatherFlights Help

For guests

You got an invite link. Now what?

Short version: open the link, type your name and add your flight. It takes a minute, and you don't need an account or an app.

  1. Open the link. Tap the whole link you were sent. It opens in your web browser on any phone or computer.

  2. Type your name. Your first name is fine. Then tap "Join the trip".

  3. Add your flight. Under "Your flights", type the flight number as it's printed on your ticket (like UA 123) and the date it leaves. Use the local date at the airport you leave from. Then tap "Add flight".

    Got a connection? Add each flight on its own. They're put in order for you.

  4. Not flying? Tap "Not flying? Driving, train, or already there" and add a rough arrival time if you like.

That's it. Following someone on their own trip instead? Type your name and tap "Follow along" instead of "Join the trip".

Who can see my flight?

  • Group trip: everyone who can see the arrivals board sees your flight. Rather not? Turn on "Hide my flight from the others". You'll show as +1, and only the organizer sees your flight.
  • Hosted trip (like a wedding): only the host sees the list.
  • Flights home are only shown to the organizer.

Something not working?

  • It says your flight wasn't found? Check the flight number and the date. Airlines publish flights about 11 months ahead, so a flight booked further out can't be added yet.
  • "This link is missing a piece": some apps cut long links short. Ask whoever sent it to send the whole link again.
  • Lost the link? Ask the organizer to send it again. If you already joined on this device, go to app.gatherflights.com and look under "Trips you joined on this device".

Changed your mind?

Tap "Leave the trip" on the trip page. Your name and flights are deleted straight away. Either way, the whole trip is deleted two days after it ends.

Your name is scrambled on your phone before it reaches us, so we can't read it. How your names stay private.
