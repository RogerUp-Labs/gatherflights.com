Privacy in the app

What we keep, and for how long

Short version: we keep the least we can, we can't read names, and we delete each trip two days after it ends.

When a trip is deleted

  • Every trip is deleted automatically two days after its last day.
  • The organizer can delete it sooner with "Delete this trip now".
  • If you tap "Leave the trip", your name and flights are deleted straight away.
  • If an organizer taps "Delete my account", every trip they run is deleted with it, straight away.

Everything we keep

"Scrambled" means it's locked on your phone or computer before it reaches us, and we don't have what it takes to unlock it.
WhatWhat we can seeWhen it's deleted
Names and trip names Nothing. They're scrambled on your device before they're sent, so all we store is scrambled text. How that works. With the trip.
Trip info cards Nothing. Stays, outings, notes and links are scrambled on your device too. With the trip.
Flights The flight number, date and airports, and its live status. We need these to follow the flight. With the trip.
Organizer's email address We don't store it. We keep a coded version that can only confirm it's you when you sign in again. There's no address to delete.
Sign-in The codes that keep you signed in are stored only in a coded form that can't be turned back into the code. With the trip, or with the organizer's account.
IP addresses and visit logs Your IP address (your internet connection's address) is held in memory for a few minutes, only to stop too many tries at once. It's never stored. The app keeps no visit logs at all. After a few minutes.
Phone alerts For people with the GatherFlights app. Each alert is locked for your phone alone, so Apple and Google, which deliver alerts to phones, see only scrambled text. With the trip.
Hosted trip extras Nothing. Pickups and drop-offs, where a guest is staying (a place name only, never an address) and the host's guest groups are all scrambled. Each guest gets back only their own ride and stay, and guest groups go only to the host. When a guest drives a pickup, we keep which guests they collect, as ID numbers with no names, so those flights show live for the driver. Each passenger can turn that off. With the trip.
Flights shared with people outside the trip In a group trip you can share just your own flights with up to 2 people outside the trip, through a private link. We keep which person each link follows. Your name and what you call them are scrambled. They need no account and never see anyone else in the trip. When you stop sharing, leave the trip, or the trip is deleted.
AI assistants Only if you approve one to help you. We keep the name it gave itself, what you let it do, and when you approved it. Nothing it reads is logged. You or the organizer can remove it any time. It stops working when the trip is deleted.
"Something wrong?" notes Only what you type and pick. Your email only if you ask for a reply. No account, trip, IP address or device ID. When we've dealt with it, or 90 days after it arrives.
After a trip is deleted One anonymous line for counting: the month, the kind of trip, the number of people and the number of flights. Nothing else. This line is what stays.
Payments Only the payment records tax law requires. As long as tax law requires.

Who else sees anything

  • Our flight information provider gets flight numbers and dates, so it can tell us how each flight is doing. It never gets names.
  • No ads. We don't sell anything about you, or share it for ads.
  • Our email service delivers the sign-in link to organizers, so it handles that address to send the email. Like any email service it keeps delivery records for a limited time, and it doesn't use them for anything else.
  • No outside analytics and no cookies.
