Private by design

Your trip is your business. We built GatherFlights to know as little about you as it can.

  • Deleted after the trip. Flights, names and the board go away when the trip ends.
  • No guest accounts. Guests join with the invite link, not a sign-up.
  • No ads, no trackers. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or anything loaded from other companies.
  • We never sell your data. Not flights, not names, not where you're headed.
